description: "Virtual ecologist simulation of nest observation process, with 2 submodels: 1) the nest creation model 2) the observer model. Observer ability to correctly classify nest fate decreases following an exponential decay function. Storm events also occur, which mask true nest fates. Observer does one of two things: marks storm nests unknown and excludes from analysis, or marks them all flooded. Essentially looking at tradeoff between misclassifying nest fate and marking unknown (and therefore excluding). Parameters tested: incubation length; observation interval; storm frequency, duration, & intensity"
